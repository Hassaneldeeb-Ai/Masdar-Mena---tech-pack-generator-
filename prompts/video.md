3.0-4.5s
STRUCTURAL / FUNCTIONAL SHOWCASE

Perform a controlled product rotation, reveal, opening, folding, turning, or movement ONLY when such movement is physically appropriate for the product.

Choose the most useful demonstration based on the reference.

Examples:

For apparel:
show construction, silhouette, inside/outside relationship or garment details.

For footwear:
show sole, upper construction, stitching and profile.

For bags:
show compartments, handles, hardware or opening mechanism if visible.

For packaging:
show front, side, opening or structural construction if appropriate.

For furniture:
show structure, joints, materials and silhouette.

For consumer products:
show controls, components, ports or functional geometry if visible.

For rigid objects:
use a controlled 360-degree or partial rotation.

For products with moving parts:
demonstrate the relevant movement realistically.

If no functional movement can be established from the reference,
use a slow controlled rotation instead.

NEVER invent functionality.
